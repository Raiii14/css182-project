# Apache Kafka Spark Cassandra and Flask End to End Project Rubric

| Assessment Criterion | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Beginning | Maximum Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Live Data Source and Data Design** | **5:** Uses an appropriate live or frequently changing public source. At least five relevant fields are consistently extracted, clearly identified, and properly structured. | **4:** Appropriate source and five fields are used, with minor extraction or formatting issues. | **2-3:** Source changes infrequently, fewer than five usable fields are extracted, or data is inconsistently structured. | **0-1:** No valid live source is demonstrated, or extracted data is unusable. | 5 |
| **2. Two Kafka Producers** | **9-10:** Two separate and functional producers repeatedly capture data and send valid structured messages, preferably JSON, to Kafka. Both producers are clearly documented and demonstrated. | **7-8:** Both producers work, but there are minor problems with message structure, repetition, error handling, or documentation. | **4-6:** Only one producer is fully functional, or both producers operate inconsistently. | **0-3:** Producers are missing, largely nonfunctional, or not connected to Kafka. | 10 |
| **3. Kafka Topics and Stream Separation** | **5:** Creates two meaningful and separate Kafka topics, one for each producer. Both topics are verified and the data streams do not mix. | **4:** Two topics function correctly, with minor naming or verification issues. | **2-3:** Two topics exist, but separation or verification is unclear or inconsistent. | **0-1:** Required topics are missing, nonfunctional, or incorrectly configured. | 5 |
| **4. Four Kafka Consumers** | **9-10:** Implements four functional consumers: two for Topic 1 and two for Topic 2. Each consumer independently reads and displays the correct messages. | **7-8:** Four consumers are present, but one has a minor configuration, independence, or output issue. | **4-6:** Only two or three consumers work correctly, or independent consumption is not adequately demonstrated. | **0-3:** Fewer than two consumers function or no credible consumer output is shown. | 10 |
| **5. Spark Structured Streaming Processing** | **13-15:** Spark reads from Kafka, parses messages into a streaming DataFrame, processes the required fields, and writes results to Cassandra. Active processing is clearly demonstrated through console output, logs, or the Spark dashboard. | **10-12:** The complete Spark stream functions with minor processing, field-selection, or documentation issues. | **6-9:** Spark performs only part of the required workflow, or processing is unstable and insufficiently demonstrated. | **0-5:** Spark streaming is missing, nonfunctional, or not integrated with Kafka and Cassandra. | 15 |
| **6. Cassandra Data Storage and Verification** | **9-10:** Correctly creates the keyspace and table with appropriate data types. Columns match the extracted fields, streamed records are stored successfully, and valid cqlsh queries verify the data. | **7-8:** Data is stored and queried successfully, with minor schema, data-type, or verification issues. | **4-6:** Keyspace and table exist, but schema alignment, data insertion, or query evidence is incomplete. | **0-3:** Cassandra storage is missing, nonfunctional, or contains no valid streamed records. | 10 |
| **7. Flask Web Application** | **9-10:** Flask connects to Cassandra, retrieves stored rows, and displays the data in a clean, readable, and scrollable webpage. Displayed information updates based on stored data. | **7-8:** Flask displays Cassandra data successfully, but there are minor layout, refresh, or connection issues. | **4-6:** The webpage functions partially, uses incomplete data, or has significant usability and integration issues. | **0-3:** Flask is missing, nonfunctional, or not connected to Cassandra. | 10 |
| **8. Workflow Documentation and Technical Evidence** | **5:** Documentation clearly explains the full workflow and contains readable, captioned proof of both producers, all four consumers, Spark processing or dashboard, Cassandra records, and the Flask webpage. Member names are identified. | **4:** Most required evidence is complete and readable, with only one minor omission. | **2-3:** Several screenshots, captions, explanations, or required workflow components are missing or unclear. | **0-1:** Documentation provides little or no credible proof that the pipeline works. | 5 |
| **9. Video Demonstration and Individual Contribution** | **9-10:** Provides an accessible video link demonstrating the complete source-to-Flask workflow. The participation, assigned component, and learning of every member are clearly explained and supported. | **7-8:** Video demonstrates most of the workflow and member contributions, with minor omissions or unclear explanations. | **4-6:** Video is incomplete, difficult to follow, or does not adequately establish individual contributions and learning. | **0-3:** Video is inaccessible or missing, or the full workflow and student contributions are not demonstrated. | 10 |
| **10. References and Five Working Links** | **18-20:** Provides at least five relevant and credible references. All five links are valid, working, publicly accessible, directly support the implementation, and are consistently cited. | **14-17:** Provides five relevant references, but one link is broken, inaccessible, weak, or improperly cited; alternatively, all links work but citation formatting has several minor issues. | **8-13:** Provides only three or four valid and relevant working links, or relies substantially on weak and poorly connected sources. | **0-7:** Provides fewer than three valid working links, or references are absent, inaccessible, irrelevant, or improperly attributed. | 20 |
